# Building an Expert-Led Content Engine at Lamoda

**Jane Goleva · Developer Relations / Technical Employer Brand · 2016–2021**

Lamoda was known as a fashion retailer. Its engineers were solving complex e-commerce and automation problems, but that expertise was barely visible to prospective candidates. My job was to turn engineering knowledge into a steady stream of useful public content.

## What I built

I worked with engineering leaders to identify topics and contributors, then created a route from an internal idea to a public talk or article. Our recurring Speakers Club gave engineers a place to develop their ideas, practise and get peer feedback. Experienced speakers became coaches for new contributors.

I secured agreement from engineering leadership to treat talk preparation as working time. The programme grew beyond conference talks into articles, podcasts and internal knowledge-sharing.

I led the programme and its editorial direction, supported contributors and wrote selected materials. Engineers supplied the technical expertise and authored or presented their specialist work.

## Result

By 2020, the programme was producing approximately 20–30 external conference talks each year, with a recurring flow of topics and contributors. An [independent interview with an engineering leader](https://habr.com/ru/articles/506308/) describes the process from the engineering team's perspective.

I also trained a junior colleague who subsequently took over the function.

The result was a content programme that drew on a network of experts rather than depending on one person to create every piece.
