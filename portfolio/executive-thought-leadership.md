# Bringing the CTO's Perspective to a Specialist Conference

**Jane Goleva · DevRelConf programme committee · December 2024**

Our Developer Relations conference regularly discussed how to work with engineering leaders, but the conversation primarily involved DevRel practitioners. I felt that an important perspective was missing: what did the CTO actually need from the function?

## My approach

I raised this editorial gap with the programme committee and proposed bringing technical leaders into the conference as contributors, not just as potential attendees.

I invited Anatoly Panov, my former manager at Avito and an experienced speaker, who was then CTO of Yandex Maps.

The premise was to explore how engineering leaders' expectations of Developer Relations evolve as a company and its communications function mature.

Anatoly supplied the executive experience, developed his own argument and delivered the talk. My contribution was identifying the missing perspective, proposing the editorial direction and inviting the expert.

## Result

The idea became an accepted session in the conference programme, bringing the engineering leadership perspective into a specialist professional discussion.

For me, commissioning expert content starts with finding a question that matters to the audience, then identifying someone with the first-hand knowledge to answer it.
